## Short post

I designed a JSON-first network assistant that separates reasoning from authority: discover, retrieve, gate, plan, confirm, apply, report. A plausible plan is not permission to touch a network.

## Thread-style post

**Opening**

Most network-agent demos jump from one observation to a proposed command. I wanted the safety boundary to be visible.

**The pipeline**

Discovery and evidence retrieval are read-only. Fixed gates cover management, physical link, topology, transport, collective communication, and workload evidence.

**The authority boundary**

Planning does not unlock apply. Confirmation is tied to one plan, rollback is mandatory, and failed or unknown gates cause refusal.

**The public version**

Every endpoint is fictional. There is no live configuration, topology, inventory, credential, or operational command here.

**The lesson**

An assistant is still useful when its correct answer is: the evidence is not sufficient to act.
